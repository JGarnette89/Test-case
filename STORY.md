# STORY.md — Red City: Miskawin Motor-Town Simulation

## Core Vision
A top-down / isometric 2D-pixel/voxel driving simulation offering an immersive "GTA in your pocket" experience. The game focuses heavily on car culture, manual driving skill, vehicle physics weight transfer, and economic progression through an underground network of human drivers.

---

## Setting & Atmosphere
* **Region:** **Miskawin** (rooted in historical Great Lakes geography, blending Indigenous phonetic naming roots with French colonial and Anglo-American settlement history).
* **Hub City:** **Red City** (a dense, rust-belt motor-town featuring heavy industrial plants, flat grid concession roads, international bridge bottlenecks, and freezing lake-effect weather).
* **Era:** **1980s–1990s Analog Era.** There are no smartphones, digital mini-maps, or instant cloud messaging. Navigation relies on paper roadmaps in the glovebox, handwritten marginalia, physical dispatch slips, CB radios, and rotary payphones.
* **The Antagonist:** **A.U.T.O. (Autonomous Infrastructure Utility / Network).** An ambient, algorithmic city-wide operating system that controls traffic lights, overhead gantry cameras, and induction loops. A.U.T.O. has replaced human driving with uniform, sterile, electric compliance pods ("toasters") that move at rigid speeds and enforce absolute predictability.

---

## Narrative Hub: Brooks Dispatch
* **The Character:** **Artie Brooks** — A weathered, old-school transit dispatcher operating out of a cinder-block office under the rusted girders of the international bridge. Surrounded by analog switchboards, coffee-ringed paperwork, and cassette tapes, Artie acts as the player's mentor and operational hub.
* **The Mechanics:** Artie runs a physical, carbon-copy dispatch board. Jobs are selected manually from paper slips, tying the player directly into the underground resistance against A.U.T.O.'s sterile infrastructure.

---

## The Connects Network (Specialists)
Players take on day and night contracts across Red City and its surrounding townships, working with five distinct local authorities:

1. **Brenda Braga (Suburbs & Exam Mode):** 
   * *Domain:* Clean suburban subdivisions and strict regulatory compliance.
   * *Focus:* Teaching precision, stopping distances, and zero-tolerance driver exam scoring.
2. **Victor Braga (Industrial Freight):** 
   * *Domain:* Industrial docks, assembly plants, and heavy manufacturing corridors.
   * *Focus:* Heavy cargo hauling, torque management, and industrial transport logistics.
3. **Hank Miller (Rural Outcounty):** 
   * *Domain:* Gravel concession roads, flat farmland, and pitch-black rural highways.
   * *Focus:* Low-traction surface handling, weight transfer on dirt, and wilderness survival driving.
4. **Maya Lin (Downtown & Nightlife):** 
   * *Domain:* The neon-soaked downtown core and club district.
   * *Focus:* Midnight street performance, tight cornering, and urban drift mechanics.
5. **Zane "Slip" Gault (Interstate Bypasses):** 
   * *Domain:* Multi-lane concrete highways and high-speed arterial loops.
   * *Focus:* High-speed highway weaving ("swimming"), slipstreaming, and dodging A.U.T.O. speed gantries at 90 mph.

---

## Core Progression Loop & Dynamic Traffic Evolution
* **Daytime (The Hustle):** Take on legal or grey-market courier runs, transport raw industrial parts, and guide nervous students through Brenda’s exam routes to earn clean cash and upgrade vehicle parts in Artie’s bay.
* **Nighttime (The Reclaim):** Hit the asphalt for high-speed runs with Zane, street tests with Maya, or long-distance hauling with Hank.
* **The World Reaction (Economic Revival):** 
  * *Early Game:* The roads are choked with lifeless, slow-moving A.U.T.O. compliance pods. Traffic is a rigid conveyor belt.
  * *Late Game:* As you complete contracts and train drivers, the local supply chain and independent chop shops revive. The traffic spawner dynamically injects varied vehicles (rusted sedans, V8 muscle cars, tuned imports) and loosens AI compliance parameters, turning the streets into a chaotic, living mosaic of human driving styles.
