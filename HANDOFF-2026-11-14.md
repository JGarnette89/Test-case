## 5. HOW WE WORKED, WHAT COST US TIME (all recorded in DECISIONS; the short list)

- **Run a gate ALONE.** A gate (8 at once, `--timeout 2400`) beside probes produced 8 false timeouts out of 16 once. Run probes alone or the gate alone, never both. `node tools/check.mjs` derives its set against the merge-base with `origin/main` and prints the base.
- **A watch needs a deadline and a liveness check, and the output must be READ directly afterwards.** Three traps: `grep " passed"` matches "assertions passed" in a SLOW line (use `^[0-9]+ of [0-9]+ passed`); "monitor expired" means the watch ended, not the work; and a probe that prints nothing within seconds of launch has FAILED (three died at launch on a shelved export and were reported as "waiting" for forty minutes).
- **Short-window sampling of the road polyline is unreliable; use a chord** (10.98): the polyline has a vertex every 4 m, so a +-3 m window spans one vertex or two and doubles and halves. The error goes as one over the chord (`tools/measure/chord-noise.mjs`); `CHORD` is 80 m. Kinks at path joins read as curvature 1/20; `BEND_JOIN` (25 m) is **load-bearing: without it 42 ordinary drivers leave the road.**
- **There is deliberately NO confidence term in the bend judgement** (10.98): under the three-factor model a confident-but-unskilled driver crashes, they are not slowed; confidence expresses itself as risk, not pace. Do not "fix" its absence.
- **`tools/scratch/` is git-ignored and NOT backed up.** The throwaway instrumented copies of `src` (`srcx`, `srcx2`, `srcx3`, `srcx4`) and the patch scripts live there and mean nothing to the next session; the measurement tools worth keeping are in `tools/measure/` (committed): `highway-wall`, `highway-sort`, `held-gain`, `held-rate`, `highway-clump`, `highway-jam`, `spawn-lanes`, `lane-speeds`, `density-met`, `density-where`, `keepright-cost`, `corner-spread`, `bends-observe`, `bends-cost`, `join-spikes`, `chord-noise`, `step-cost-map`.
