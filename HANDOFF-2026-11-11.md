## The rulings this task produced (the maintainer's, relayed)

- **10.68: the traffic perceives by geometry, not lane membership.** A car on no lane is visible and avoided; nobody yields to it (it has no right of way); the ghost goes. Reasoning, to keep: a model where traffic perceives only lane-assigned cars has no hazards in it, and it is the same principle as the sight/occlusion work.
- **10.69: fault follows avoidability, not position.** "If you can see and reasonably react to an obstacle and fail to do so that's on the driver." It generalises the 29 September ruling (an unavoidable collision must not punish the player). **Checked by reading: visibility is reachable where fault is decided; avoidability is NOT computed there** (`contact.js` decides by `owedBy`, `cutIn`, `rearStruck`; the arithmetic exists once in `peds.js` `playerCanRespond`, asked before an event). **`rearStruck` assigns fault by POSITION today (more than 0.9 m off the lane's line = the player's doing), which the ruling contradicts.** One quantity is missing: when the obstacle became seeable to the striker. Not designed.
- **Lanes only (the project lead):** the box is not covered. A car on no lane inside an intersection is seen by NOBODY there now (its ghost was at least somewhere).
