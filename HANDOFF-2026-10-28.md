## The finding most likely to be rediscovered: self-occlusion

**A driver's eye sits 1.5 m ahead of the vehicle centre (peds.js
`eyeOf`) — in front of a car, but INSIDE a 9 m truck. The pedestrian
sight test counted every box taller than 2 m as an occluder, the viewer's
own included, so every truck driver was blind to mid-block crossings.**
Invisible to every instrument we had; found only by asking whether the
struck pedestrian was absent from the driver's consideration or present
and filtered (present, held by the near-half rule, filtered by sight).
Fixed in `e4e9191`: `inSight` leaves the viewer's own body out. SIMULATOR
IN FLIGHT 33, DECISIONS 10.23 item 11.

**Open question for whoever picks this up — do not go looking unasked:**
does anything else in the sight path have the same shape? Any test where a
vehicle could occlude itself (crossing.js has its own `eyeOf` for truck
sight lines, sight.js `hiddenFrom`), or an offset safe for a car and wrong
for a longer vehicle. Buses are 12 m.

### 2. The traffic skill dial and the per-driver readout (Jay's request)

Jay: *"the traffic is actually very polite, I'm wondering if we have made
the average driver too good. is there a way I could actually change the
skill of the traffic while testing? like a dial for different skills.
also being able to see the skill level of the driver you are observing
would help."*

**Build both, framed as a FALSIFICATION TEST, not a convenience**: can a
player tell a poorly rated driver from a well-rated one by watching? It
was only ever tested on the old side-by-side candidate screen, never in
live traffic, and the whole driver model rests on the answer. If Jay turns
the dial down and the road does not visibly get worse, that is the most
important finding available.

- **The dial** shifts the population's rating distribution while driving,
  in #/map's debug controls beside the existing toggles. It must actually
  MOVE the distribution (where `composeDriver` in core/driver.js draws
  WEAK/SOUND ranges and how many weak axes), not clamp it. Population, not
  physics (DECISIONS 10.30 point 1).
- **The readout**: tap or select a car, see its FIVE AXES (and compliance)
  as they are — never a summary score. Jay needs to see WHICH axis is low
  in a driver behaving badly.
